Saturday, 18 September 2027 — Cultural programme

A guided programme on the history of libraries, manuscripts and book culture in Istanbul. Preliminary itinerary:

  • 09:00
    Departure by coach from Rami Library
  • 09:45–11:15
    Süleymaniye Manuscript Library and the Süleymaniye complex
  • 11:30–12:30
    Beyazıt State Library and the Sahaflar (second-hand book) market
  • 12:30–14:00
    Lunch (own expense)
  • 14:00–15:30
    Sultanahmet: Hagia Sophia and the Topkapı Palace Library area (guided walk)
  • 16:00–18:00
    Bosphorus boat tour; return to the historic peninsula

The cultural programme is open to registered full-conference participants. Transport and guide are provided by the organisers; lunch and entrance fees are at participants' own expense. Places are limited and are allocated in order of registration. Conditions will be confirmed when registration opens.

Session formats
  • Keynote lecture: 60 min
  • Paper session: 3 × (20 + 10 min)
  • Workshop: 90 min, max. 25 participants
  • Panel: 90 min
  • Research in progress: 10 + 5 min
  • Poster: A0 portrait
Included in the fee
  • Scientific programme
  • Conference materials
  • Coffee breaks on both days
  • Lunch on 16 and 17 September
  • Welcome reception

Conference dinner: €55, booked with registration.
