Important Dates

All deadlines expire at 23:59 Istanbul time (UTC+3). Any change will be announced on this page.

Call for papers

  • 15 December 2026Call for papers and abstract submission system open
  • 18 April 2027Abstract submission deadline (all formats)
  • 21 May 2027Notification of acceptance
  • 14 July 2027Registration deadline for presenting authors
  • 31 August 2027Publication of the final programme and the Book of Abstracts

  • 10 May – 14 July 2027Early registration (€250 full; €150 students and PhD researchers)

  • Friday, 17 September 2027Conference day 2: keynote lecture, sessions, poster session, closing; conference dinner (optional)
