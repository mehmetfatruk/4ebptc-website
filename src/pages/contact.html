Sponsorship and exhibition

Publishers, training institutions, associations and companies active in the fields of libraries, reading, mental health and wellbeing may support the conference as sponsors or exhibitors. Options include exhibition tables, inserts in the conference materials, sponsorship of coffee breaks, the welcome reception or the conference dinner, and support for student bursaries. The sponsorship document is available from the secretariat at info@4ebptc.org.tr.
